Locksmith – Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · App: Locksmith (Android, package com.jonathangames.locksmith) · Developer: Jonathan (Jonathan Games)

In short
  • Locksmith has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress (level, coins, tools) is stored only on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device’s advertising ID and related data to show and measure ads.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in ⚙ Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Locksmith saves your progress (level, coins, tools you bought, daily-job streak, sound setting) in the app’s private storage on your phone so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app, or using ⚙ Settings → Reset progress, deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads, Google’s software in the app may collect and process:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable ID from your device)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, personalisation where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from itShowing ads suitable for your country and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads watched)Measurement, rewards in the game, fraud prevention

This data is collected and handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive this data in a form that identifies you; AdMob only gives the developer totals such as the number of ads shown and earnings.

Your choices: you can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → All services → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form, and you can reopen it at ⚙ Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

3. Other connections the app makes

  • Game updates: when the app opens, it downloads the newest version of the game page from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address. No game data is sent.
  • Fonts: the game may load its font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which also receives your IP address.
  • Clipboard: only when you tap “Copy my result to share” does the app put text on your clipboard. It never reads your clipboard.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not ask for permissions to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

5. Children

Locksmith is a general puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are requested without personalisation where the law requires it. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security and retention

Game progress stays on your phone until you delete it. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google’s retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the Data Protection Act 2021 in Zambia), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the developer does not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google, or through your phone’s ad settings. You can still contact us with any question.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
